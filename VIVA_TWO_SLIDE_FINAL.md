### **💎 SLIDE 2: TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & PERFORMANCE**
**"The Hybrid Transformer Ensemble: Neural Intelligence Meets Hydrology"**

*   **The Master Data**: We developed a **6-Channel Spectral Dataset** (RGB + NDWI). Concatenating spectral water reflectance with visual pixels helps the AI "see" through mud and forest shadows.
*   **The AI Trinity (Transformer Ensemble)**:
    1.  **SigLIP**: Vision-Language "Gatekeeper" that validates the environment with 98.89% accuracy.
    2.  **SegFormer**: Hierarchical Transformer for pixel-perfect flood segmentation across any resolution.
    3.  **YOLO-World**: Zero-Shot detector for dynamic hazards (Crocodiles/People) using natural language prompts.
*   **Performance Mastery**: The entire pipeline is optimized for **Apple Silicon (MPS GPU)**, running real-time at **15-20 FPS**.
*   **Explainable AI (XAI)**: We use **Grad-CAM heatmaps** to visualize the AI's internal attention, ensuring transparent and trustworthy decision-making.
